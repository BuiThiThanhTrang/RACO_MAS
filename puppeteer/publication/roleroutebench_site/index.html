Why RoleRouteBench?

Final task success cannot tell you whether a router coordinated well.

Outcome quality also depends on the frozen executor. RoleRouteBench therefore retains every routing decision, failed action, state transition, budget event, and independent evaluator result.

Competing routers see the same public state, role pool, adapters, executors, budgets, and evaluator. The environment stays fixed; only the routing policy changes.

Evaluation boundary

Public/private isolation by construction

“Private” means evaluator-only under the benchmark protocol; the artifacts remain downloadable for transparent reproduction.

Reference state
